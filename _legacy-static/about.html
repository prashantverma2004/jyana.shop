About JYANA

Less, but better made.

JYANA Personal Care is a focused personal-care brand. We make two roll-ons — and we'd rather do two things well than many things halfway.

Our idea

Everyday care, without the noise.

The bathroom shelf has become crowded. Endless variants, long lists of claims, and packaging that shouts. JYANA started from the opposite direction: what does someone actually need to feel fresh and confident, every single day?

The answer was two roll-ons. An everyday essential made for all skin types, and a natural deodorant roll-on for active days. Each one is clear about what it does, and quietly beautiful enough to leave out on the shelf.
